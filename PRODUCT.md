# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Parents and guardians in Little Rock, Arkansas who want basketball skills training for athletes from grade school through professional level. The public site helps new families understand the program and book a first session. Returning families use a connected member portal to reserve open training times, manage bookings, and see payment due dates.

## Product Purpose

CRM2 Skillz & Drillz turns interest into a confirmed training session with a short, parent-friendly booking flow. It also gives existing customers one dependable place to schedule, reschedule, cancel, view deposit status, and pay through Venmo. Success means fewer scheduling messages for the coach and fewer unclear steps for parents.

## Brand Personality

Disciplined, energetic, and grounded. The voice is direct and encouraging, with the visual confidence of a well-run basketball program rather than a generic youth activity website.

## Anti-references

Avoid soft daycare styling, corporate fitness templates, flashy sports-betting visuals, crowded dashboards, and complicated multi-step forms. The experience must not look childish, overly technical, or payment-heavy.

## Design Principles

1. Make the next action unmistakable for busy parents.
2. Show real athletes and real training before making marketing claims.
3. Treat the $25 deposit policy as a clear agreement, not fine print.
4. Keep public discovery bold and expressive while keeping the member portal calm and task-focused.
5. Use one source of truth for availability, bookings, payment due dates, and confirmations.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Maintain readable contrast, keyboard access, visible focus states, descriptive labels, reduced-motion support, and plain-language instructions. Forms must be usable on a phone with large tap targets and errors that explain how to recover.
